The public showcase now uses a completely rebuilt **45-second vertical product film** based on the current PAUSE AI mobile interface. It follows the real journey from receiver-controlled notification setup and app allowlisting through Deep Work, a held synthetic notification, Vault review, Digest, and fail-closed Privacy controls.

The film separates AI advice from the deterministic policy action and keeps manual control visible. Every notification identity and preview in the film is synthetic; no teammate or production-user data appears in the render.

The soundtrack and interface sound effects were generated specifically for PAUSE AI. Playback remains user-controlled with no autoplay, tracking, or browser-side backend connection.
